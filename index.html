Prosta Peak™ Ingredients

Prosta Peak™ is a dietary supplement formulated with a blend of botanical ingredients designed to support prostate health, urinary function, and men's overall wellness. Its current product website highlights six key natural ingredients. These plant-based ingredients are traditionally used in men's wellness supplements and provide various nutritional compounds.

1

Saw Palmetto

Saw Palmetto is one of the most commonly used botanical ingredients in prostate health supplements. It comes from the berries of the Serenoa repens palm tree. It contains naturally occurring fatty acids and plant compounds and is traditionally used to support normal prostate function and urinary wellness. However, clinical research has not consistently demonstrated benefits for urinary symptoms.
2

Pygeum

Pygeum is an herbal extract obtained from the bark of the African cherry tree. It has a long history of traditional use in men's health formulations. It contains plant compounds that are being studied for their potential role in supporting normal urinary function and prostate wellness.
3

Green Tea

Green tea is derived from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant. It contains natural antioxidants called catechins, including EGCG. These compounds help protect cells from oxidative stress and contribute to general wellness. Green tea is also valued for its potential contribution to healthy metabolic and cellular functions.
4

Cat's Claw

Cat's Claw is a traditional botanical obtained from a tropical climbing vine. It contains naturally occurring plant compounds, including alkaloids and polyphenols. It is commonly included in herbal wellness formulations because of its antioxidant properties and potential role in supporting general immune health.
5

Raspberry

Raspberry is a nutritious fruit containing naturally occurring antioxidants, vitamins, and plant compounds. It provides polyphenols and other beneficial nutrients that contribute to general nutritional wellness. Its antioxidant content makes it a useful addition to plant-based supplement formulations.
6

Soursop

Soursop, also known as Graviola, is a tropical fruit traditionally used in different herbal preparations. It contains naturally occurring plant compounds and antioxidants. It is included in some botanical wellness formulas, although reliable human evidence supporting prostate-specific benefits remains limited.
